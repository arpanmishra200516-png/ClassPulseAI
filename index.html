Core Architecture & Technical Specifications

👁️

Cumulative Presence Attendance

Replaces flawed single-frame scans with accumulated time observation. Requires $\ge 60\%$ verified presence with temporal gap-tolerance merging.

🎙️

Acoustic Floor & VAD

Computes RMS signal energy and a rolling 10th-percentile noise floor unaffected by loud speech, coupled with hangover buffering.

🛡️

100% Privacy-Preserving

Uses IoU/Centroid anonymous tracking (Participant_01). No biometric face encodings or identifiable photos are ever stored.

📊

Explainable Engagement Scoring

Transparent 0–100 heuristic fusion balancing visual presence (40%), natural movement (25%), and acoustic dialogue (35%).
