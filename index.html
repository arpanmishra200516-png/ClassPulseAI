› System Architecture, Educational Mission & Privacy Safeguards ▾

ClassPulse AI operates entirely on local hardware without cloud data transmission, external API fees, or proprietary models. Visual tracking assigns temporary anonymous IDs (Participant_01, Participant_02) without retaining biometric facial templates or identity databases, ensuring full compliance with FERPA & GDPR standards.

Engagement heuristics compute normalized physical presence ($S_{pres}$), micro-motion dynamics ($S_{mov}$), and acoustic speech SNR ($S_{aud}$), translating raw observations into non-prescriptive, explainable teaching recommendations.
